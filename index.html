Battivra

Your iPhone's battery, on your Apple Watch.

The last reported level

See your iPhone's battery level and whether it is charging, right on your wrist.

Always honest about age

Every reading shows how old it is. Open Battivra on your Apple Watch and it asks your iPhone for a new one.

Both ways

Your iPhone shows your Apple Watch's battery too, while Battivra is open on the watch.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no analytics. Readings stay on your iPhone and Apple Watch and are never sent to a server.

Requires an iPhone with iOS 26 or later and a paired Apple Watch with watchOS 26 or later.
