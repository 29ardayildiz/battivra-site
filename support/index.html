Requirements

An iPhone with iOS 26 or later and a paired Apple Watch with watchOS 26 or later, with Battivra installed on both.

Common questions

How do I see my iPhone's battery on my Apple Watch?

Open Battivra on your Apple Watch. It shows the last level your iPhone reported and asks your iPhone for a new one. You can also tap Refresh.

Why doesn't the value update on its own?

iOS and watchOS don't let apps send battery updates continuously in the background. Battivra shows the last reported reading and how old it is. Readings older than 30 minutes are marked "Last known". Opening the app on your Apple Watch asks for a new reading.

Why does it say "~55%" instead of the exact percentage?

iOS and watchOS give apps the battery level in steps of five percent, so Battivra shows it with a ~ sign. The status bar may differ by a few percent.

My Apple Watch says "iPhone unavailable".

Your iPhone is out of range, or its Bluetooth and Wi-Fi are off. Battivra keeps showing the last known level and its age. Bring your devices together and tap Refresh.

How do I see my Apple Watch's battery on my iPhone?

Your iPhone receives the Apple Watch level while Battivra is open on the watch. Pull down on Battivra's home screen on your iPhone to ask for a new reading.

Battivra isn't on my Apple Watch.

On your iPhone, open the Watch app, go to My Watch, scroll to Available Apps and tap Install next to Battivra.

Is there a watch face complication or a charging alert?

Not in this version.

Does Battivra collect my data?

No. See the Privacy Policy.
